About the Division

Trusted Timber Supplier
in Tirunelveli

St. Michael Timber Depot & Sawmill is the flagship industrial division of the St. Michael Group of Companies. Operating out of Palayamkottai, we specialize in sourcing premium imported hardwoods from across the globe and processing them through our industrial sawmill facility.

From raw timber logs to precision-cut door frames, window frames, furniture woods, and customized construction timber — every piece is graded, processed, and delivered to the specifications our clients demand. We supply builders, architects, churches, schools, and institutions across South Tamil Nadu.

I

Global Import Network

Direct-sourced timber from Ghana, Togo, Burma, Benin, Thailand, Brazil, Australia, Panama & Nigeria — ensuring quality at origin.

S

Industrial Sawmill Operations

On-site sawmill for precision cutting, sizing, and preparation of timber to exact specifications for any application.

C

Customized Wood Solutions

From standard construction timber to intricate interior woodwork — bespoke fabrication tailored to project requirements.

How We Work

From Source to Site

Every piece of timber passes through our four-stage process — from global sourcing to final delivery on your project site.

01

Global Sourcing

We source directly from premium timber regions across 9 countries — Ghana, Burma, Brazil, Thailand and more — ensuring quality at the point of origin.

02

Sawmill Processing

Our industrial sawmill facility processes raw logs into precise cut sizes — beams, planks, frames, and custom sections — to exact project specifications.

03

Grading & Quality

Every batch is visually graded for knots, grain consistency, moisture content, and structural integrity before leaving our yard.

04

Delivery to Site

Timber is transported directly to your construction site across South Tamil Nadu — on time, properly packed, and to the dimensions you ordered.
