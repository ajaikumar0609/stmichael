The Process

How Chit Funds Work

A chit fund is a simple, time-tested savings instrument. Here's how the St. Michael Chits process works from registration to receiving your funds.

📝
Register & Join

Choose your chit plan, complete the KYC process, and join a chit group with fellow subscribers.

💳
Pay Instalments

Pay your fixed monthly (or weekly) instalment. Receive an official receipt for every payment made.

🔨
Monthly Auction

Every month, subscribers bid for the chit amount. The person who bids the lowest discount wins the prize.

💰
Receive Funds

The winner receives the chit amount minus the discount. Non-winners get a dividend share from the discount pool.

✅
Cycle Completes

After all months are complete, every subscriber has received funds once. A win for every member.
